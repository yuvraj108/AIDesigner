VitalLink web application

VitalLink Clinical OS is a centralized healthcare platform that simplifies staffing, compliance and financial operations for medical facilities, helping administrators manage everything in one place.

Problem

What slowed administrators down

01

Too many systems

Administrators had to switch between multiple tools to manage staffing, credentials, compliance and finances, making daily operations slow and difficult.

02

Limited real-time visibility

Without a single view of critical data, it was difficult to spot staffing gaps, compliance risks and financial issues quickly.

Solution

What I designed

01

One centralized platform

Combined key healthcare operations into one dashboard, making information easier to access and manage.

02

Faster, more confident decisions

Real-time insights and automated tracking helped administrators identify issues faster and manage operations with greater confidence.

Bio

Daniel runs staffing, compliance and budgets for a busy hospital. He spends too much time pulling numbers from different systems and finds problems only after they grow.

Goals

  • See staffing, compliance and spend in one place
  • Fill urgent shifts quickly
  • Stay ready for every audit
  • Keep spending within budget

Needs

  • Live numbers on one screen
  • Early alerts for staffing gaps
  • Clear view of expiring credentials
  • Payout records that are easy to check

Frustrations

  • Switching between many tools
  • Staffing gaps found too late
  • Expired licences found by surprise
  • Spend reports that arrive late
