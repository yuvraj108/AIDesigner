VitalLink mobile app design

A healthcare staffing app that helps clinicians find flexible shifts, track their earnings and manage their credentials, all in one place.

The result

Clinicians can find a shift, accept it and see their pay in a few taps.

  • Flexible shiftsPick work that fits around life.
  • Clear payRate, earnings and payouts always visible.
  • Credentials in one placeFacilities can trust and book faster.

Problem

What made shift work hard

01

Scattered messages

Shift offers and changes come by calls, texts and emails, so details get missed.

02

Unclear pay

It’s hard to see what a shift pays, or when the money will arrive.

03

Fixed schedules

Shifts are hard to change, so fitting work around life is difficult.

04

Slow, unsure hiring

Facilities need trusted staff quickly, but checking credentials takes time.

Solution

What I designed

01

Find flexible shifts

Recommended shifts show the rate, place and date, and can be accepted in one tap.

02

Clear earnings

Balance, weekly pay and every payment in one view, with bank details close by.

03

Credentials in one place

A profile with certification and status, so facilities can trust and book faster.
